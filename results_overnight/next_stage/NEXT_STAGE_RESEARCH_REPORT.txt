Interpretation
--------------
The episode-level analysis is intended to determine whether the previously identified window-level relationship forms distinct recurring episodes. Overlapping windows are not treated as independent observations.

The control comparison is descriptive and does not by itself establish a causal relationship or a new detector coupling.

A relationship with auxiliary channels does not establish an astrophysical origin. Channel provenance and known detector control pathways must be examined before any novelty claim is made.
